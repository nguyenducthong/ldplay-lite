LDPlayer Lite Manager - Compatibility Edition
=============================================

Ban nay khong su dung Qt/PySide6, vi vay khong the gap loi QtCore.dll.
May dich khong can cai Python.

CACH CHAY
1. Giai nen TOAN BO file ZIP vao mot thu muc moi.
2. Chay LDPlayerLiteManager-Dark.exe.
3. Neu phan mem chua tim thay LDPlayer, vao tab Cai dat va chon thu muc LDPlayer.

Khong chay EXE truc tiep ben trong file ZIP va khong chep rieng file EXE ra
khoi thu muc nay. Thu muc _internal phai nam canh file EXE.

YEU CAU
- Windows 10/11 64-bit.
- LDPlayer 9 da duoc cai dat.
- Quyen Administrator chi can khi Windows/LDPlayer yeu cau cho mot thao tac cu the.

DU LIEU CA NHAN
Cac cau hinh da luu nam trong:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\LDPlayerLiteManager

Muc tieu ung dung duoc bao ve trong trinh quan ly goi:
  vn.kvtm.js
